## Import checklist

For each future NASA dataset, record `source_url`, `publisher`, `retrieved_at_utc`, `license_or_terms`, `version`, `hash`, `measurement_units`, `coverage`, `limitations`, and `verification_status`. Only label verified NASA sources as NASA data. No invented observations, uncited medical articles or placeholder datasets may masquerade as authentic NASA data.
