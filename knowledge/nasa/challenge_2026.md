## Original Challenge Prompt

> Long-duration missions expose astronauts to space radiation, isolation and confinement, altered gravity, and a hostile closed environment, which can bring immune changes, bone loss, cardiovascular events, and behavioral health problems. On long missions, astronauts carry much of the responsibility for spotting these changes in themselves. Your challenge is to build a health monitoring software that gathers health indicators and enables astronauts to evaluate and act on the status of their health.

## NASA HRP Five Hazards of Human Spaceflight

1. Space radiation
2. Isolation and confinement
3. Distance from Earth
4. Gravity fields
5. Hostile closed environments

## Mentioned Health Risks & Example Indicators

- **Health risks:** immune system changes, bone density loss, cardiovascular events, inadequate nutrition, behavioral health conditions.
- **Example indicators:** blood pressure, heart rate, blood sugar (`mg/dL`), body temperature, daily sleep hours, mood, ultrasound images.

## Key Objectives & Questions

1. Which measurements can be performed in space?
2. Why is an exam needed?
3. How do spaceflight hazards affect these health indicators?
4. When might a result warrant attention from the crew or from flight surgeons on the ground?
5. What actions can be taken to prevent a critical health condition?
